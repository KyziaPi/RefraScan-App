About the Study

Project Overview

Title: An EfficientNetB3-Based Deep Learning Clinical Decision Support Application for Fundus Image-Based Refractive Error Classification

A clinical decision support tool intended to assist medical professionals by providing pre-screening results for a more efficient assessment of refractive errors in the Philippine setting

Clinical Objective

1. Classify fundus images into Myopia, Hyperopia, or Normal Vision.

2. Integrate Spatial Attention mechanisms to increase diagnostic transparency.

3. Provide a deployable screening tool utilizing fundus images as a multi-purpose pre-screening tool.

Technical Framework

Architecture: EfficientNetB3

Dataset: PAPILA and The Lasik Surgery Clinic - AUFMC

Framework: Flask and Tensorflow
